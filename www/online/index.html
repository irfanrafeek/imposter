How it works

What is an online impostor game?

It is the Impostor Word Game played with people you have not met. Everyone gets the same secret word, except the impostor, who only gets a hint. There is no voice or video. Players take turns writing one clue on a shared board, then everyone votes for the player they think is the impostor. Every step has a clock, so the game keeps moving and nobody waits for a host.

How it works

From the list to the vote

  1. 1

    Pick a game

    Join a game from the list, or start your own. Pick a name and you are in. The game starts when the lobby clock runs out, with at least 3 players.

  2. 2

    Get your word

    Most players see the secret word. One player is the impostor and sees only a hint.

  3. 3

    Write one clue

    Take turns writing one clue on the board. Show that you know the word without giving it away.

  4. 4

    Vote

    Read the board and vote for the impostor. Then the game goes back to the lobby for the next round.

FAQ

How is an online game different from a private one?

A private game is for people who have your code. An online game is listed on this page, so anyone can find it and join. Online games also run on their own: the lobby, the vote and the results each have a clock.

How many players do you need?

At least 3, and up to 20. If the lobby clock runs out with fewer than 3 players, the game closes.

Is it free?

Yes. No payment, no sign-up and no account. Pick a name and play.

Is there an app to download?

No. It runs in your phone's browser, on iPhone and Android, with nothing to install.

Can I play on one phone?

Not in an online game, where everyone plays on their own screen. For one phone, create a game in the Impostor Word Game and pick Pass the Phone in the lobby.

Why is the list empty?

Only a few games run at the same time. Start one: it shows up here straight away, and anyone who opens this page can join you.

Can I join a game that is already playing?

Not in the middle of a round. You can wait for its next round and join as soon as the current one ends.

Can I play only with my friends?

Yes. Create a game, choose Private and share the code with your friends.
