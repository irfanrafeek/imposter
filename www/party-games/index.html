Impostor Games

Free online party games you can play without downloading anything

Everyone is here, everyone has a phone, and nobody wants to spend the next ten minutes installing something. This is the list for that situation.

The short version: impostorgames.com has three free party games that run in any phone browser. Nothing to install, no account to make, no ads, 3 to 20 players. One person creates a room and shares a 4-character code or a QR code, everyone else joins from their own phone, and the first round starts in about a minute.

The three games

They share a shape. Everyone gets the same thing except one person, the impostor, who gets something slightly different and has to bluff their way through without being caught. What changes between them is what your group needs to have on hand and how loud you are willing to be.

Impostor Word Game

Everyone is dealt the same secret word. The impostor gets a vague one-word hint instead, and does not know what the real word is. You go round the circle and each person says exactly one word out loud as a clue. Say something too obvious and you hand it to the impostor; say something too vague and the group starts suspecting you.

Seven categories, 550 words in total, and every word carries two possible hints so the same word plays differently the second time it comes round. Needs nothing but phones, which is why it is the one to start with. Play the Impostor Word Game.

Impostor Draw Game

Same idea, but everyone draws the word instead of describing it. One shared canvas, one turn at a time, each player in their own colour, and nobody can draw out of turn. You have to add enough to prove you know the word without spelling it out for the impostor, who is trying to fake a convincing line for a picture they cannot identify yet.

Four categories and 350 words, turns capped at 45 seconds, and the host sets between one and five rounds in the lobby. Voting opens by itself once the last turn is taken. Play the Impostor Draw Game.

Impostor Dance Game

The loud one, and the one that went viral. Everyone puts on headphones and hears the same song, except the impostor, who gets a completely different track. Everyone dances to what they can hear, and the group works out whose moves do not match the song.

This is the browser version of the Imposter Dance Challenge. Song categories run from today's pop through 80s and 90s hits to TikTok and Reels, Bollywood, Tamil and Malayalam. The catch is real: every single player needs their own headphones, so check that before you pick this one. Play the Impostor Dance Game.

What you actually need

Very little, which is the point.

  • A phone each. Any phone with a browser. iPhone, Android, or a laptop if you would rather. Nothing gets installed.
  • Three people minimum, twenty maximum. Below three there is nobody to accuse.
  • Headphones, for the dance game only. The other two need nothing.

The joining flow is the part people expect to be harder than it is. One person opens the game and taps Create. They get a 4-character room code and a QR code. Everyone else opens the same site on their own phone, taps Join, and types the four characters in, or points a camera at the QR code. That is the whole setup. No accounts, no invites, no email addresses, nothing to confirm.

Because it is all browser-based, it works over a video call too. The word and draw games are fine remotely. The dance game is better in person, since half the fun is watching people who cannot hear each other.

Why browser games and not apps

An app is fine when everyone already has it. The problem is the ten minutes when they do not: someone is out of storage, someone has forgotten their app store password, someone is on a version that will not connect to everybody else's. By the time the group is assembled, the energy that made someone suggest a game in the first place has usually gone.

A browser game skips all of it, and the trade is real: no progression, no cosmetics, nothing to unlock, and no playing with strangers on the internet. These are built for people who are already together, or already on a call. If that is your situation, the trade is a good one.

Common questions

What are the best free online party games that need no download?

The three at impostorgames.com run entirely in a phone browser: word, draw and dance. All are free, none need an account, and all work for 3 to 20 players. The word game is the easiest one to start with because it needs nothing but phones.

Can we play without making an account?

Yes. There is no sign-up at all. Nobody enters an email address, and the site does not use cookies or collect personal data.

How many people do we need?

At least 3 and up to 20, each on their own phone. Six is a good number for all three games. Below three there are not enough people for the accusations to go anywhere.

Do these work over a video call?

The word and draw games do, and they are designed to. The dance game works best with everyone in the same place, because watching people react to a song they cannot all hear is most of the point.

Is there anything to pay for?

No. No paid tier, no in-app purchases, no ads.

How long does it take to get started?

About a minute. One person taps Create and reads out a 4-character code, or shows the QR code. Everyone else taps Join and enters it. There is nothing to install and nothing to confirm.

Which one should we play first?

The word game, unless somebody has already brought headphones for everybody. It needs the least setup, the rules explain themselves in a sentence, and it works just as well round a table as it does on a call.
