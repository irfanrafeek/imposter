Token set

Every :root on the site, read out of the files that declare them. The first is the system. The other three are what one surface adds or overrides on top of it, and they are here because a local token is invisible everywhere else.

www/shared/tokens.css

The system. Every page links this before anything else, and a page that does not renders with every var() invalid.
