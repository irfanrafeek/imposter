How to Play

One canvas, one faker, and no one is safe

Impostor Draw Game (also called Impostor Draw) is a drawing party game you can play from anywhere. Everyone gets the same secret word to draw except one impostor, who only sees a vague hint. Players take turns adding to one shared canvas, watching the picture build, then everyone votes on who the faker is. Play free at impostorgames.com/draw with 3 to 20 friends, no app and no sign-up.

  3. 3

FAQ

What is the Impostor Draw Game?

A free online drawing party game for 3–20 players. Everyone secretly gets the same word to draw except one impostor, who only sees a vague hint. Players take turns on one shared canvas; the impostor fakes their strokes while working out the word, and everyone else tries to prove they know it without making it obvious.

Can I play the Impostor Draw Game remotely?

Yes, it's built for it. The canvas and the vote both live in the app, so nobody needs to be in the same room. It works well alongside a video call, or entirely on its own.

How many players do I need?

At least 3, up to 20. One person creates the room and shares the 4-character code or QR code; everyone else joins from their own device.

Is it free to play?

Yes, completely free. There's no sign-up, no app to install, and no payment. Open the page in any browser and share a room code.

Do I need to be good at drawing?

No. Rough doodles are the whole point. You only need enough detail to show you know the word, and drawing it too clearly just helps the impostor work it out.

What words come up?

Four categories: Food, Animals, Everyday Objects and Super Heroes, 350 words in all. The host picks which ones are in play, and a random word is dealt each round. Every word carries two vague one-word hints, and the impostor is shown one of them at random. That hint is all they get.
