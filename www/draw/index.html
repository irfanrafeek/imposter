How to Play

One canvas, one faker, and no one is safe

Impostor Draw Game (also called Impostor Draw) is a drawing party game you can play from anywhere. Everyone gets the same secret word to draw except one impostor, who only sees a vague hint. Players take turns adding to one shared canvas, watching the picture build, then everyone votes on who the faker is. Play free at impostorgames.com/draw with 3 to 20 friends, no app and no sign-up.

  1. 1

    Get started

    Everyone has a phone? Join the same room and tap “I’m Ready.”

    Only one phone? Enter everyone's names and pass it around.

  2. 2

    Get your word

    Most players get the same word. The Impostor only gets a hint.

  3. 3

    Start drawing

    Take turns drawing. If you're the Impostor, fake your drawing and try to blend in.

  4. 4

    Find the Impostor

    After the last round, vote for who you think the Impostor is. On one phone, talk it out and reveal.

FAQ

What is the Impostor Draw Game?

A free online drawing party game for 3–20 players. Most players get the same secret word, while the impostor only gets a vague hint. Everyone takes turns drawing on a shared online canvas, including the impostor, who tries to blend in without knowing the secret word. At the end, everyone votes to find the impostor.

Can I play the Impostor Draw Game remotely?

Yes, it's built for it. The canvas and the vote both live in the app, so nobody needs to be in the same room. It works well alongside a video call, or entirely on its own.

How many players do I need?

At least 3, up to 20. With everyone on their own phone, one person creates the room and shares the 4-character code or QR code. In Pass the Phone you need no second device at all, just the names of everyone playing.

Is it free to play?

Yes, completely free. There's no sign-up, no app to install, and no payment. Open the page in any browser and share a room code.

Do I need to be good at drawing?

No. Rough doodles are the whole point. You only need enough detail to show you know the word, and drawing it too clearly just helps the impostor work it out.

Can I play on one device?

Yes. Pick Pass the Phone in the lobby and one phone does the whole game, so nobody else needs a device and there's no room code. The phone goes round twice: once so each player can swipe their card over and see the word, or the impostor hint, in private, then once per drawing turn, handed straight to the next player with the canvas already live. There's no timer, because nobody can disappear from a phone that's being handed to them, and the turn strip names whose go it is. At the end there's no secret ballot, since that would need a screen each: the finished drawing is held up on a Find the Impostor screen, the group argues it out loud, and someone taps Reveal. The default mode, Everyone has a Phone, still gives each player their own screen.

What words come up?

Four categories: Food, Animals, Everyday Objects and Super Heroes, 350 words in all. The host picks which ones are in play, and a random word is dealt each round. Every word carries two vague one-word hints, and the impostor is shown one of them at random. That hint is all they get.
